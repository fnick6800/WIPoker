Four predefined bases produce six instruction styles:
f1 Vivian, f2 Serena, f3 Serena with a mature/lower/rougher style target;
m1 Dylan, m2 Dylan with a bolder/mid-low style target, m3 Uncle_Fu.
These are not six independent trained speakers. No audio pitch shifting,
speaker blending or reference-person voice cloning is performed.
Style and emotion instructions are synthesis targets, not auditory acceptance.

Each clip has its own semantics/intensity and pronunciation instructions.
Qwen exposes text plus instruction, not hard IPA/pinyin phoneme conditioning.
The separate audited Kokoro token dictionary is a fallback/audit reference;
it is not passed as phoneme input to Qwen. ASR cannot establish tone correctness.
AAC-LC mono 32 kHz 64 kbps, measured loudness/peak finishing and pitch-preserving
time-stretch correction are recorded in guandan-voices-v2.json.

The original 700 Kokoro assets and original manifest remain as a rollback.

Twelve new reference voices were synthesized from fictional age/personality
instructions, then reused unchanged for each identity through Base voice cloning.
No human reference recordings, celebrity imitation, pitch shifting or speaker
vector blends were used. Four predefined bases plus twelve designed identities
produce eighteen style IDs; these are not eighteen independent trained speakers.
Original six styles support per-clip CustomVoice instructions. New Base clips use
fixed designed references and text punctuation; Base does not expose the same
per-clip instruction interface. Style, age, emotion and pronunciation are targets,
not verified subjective acceptance. All final compressed files have hashes and
objective decoding/loudness measurements in guandan-voices-v2.json.
