# WIPoker · Guandan teamwork update

[English](https://github.com/fnick6800/WIPoker/blob/main/README.md) · [简体中文](https://github.com/fnick6800/WIPoker/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md) · [Download APK](https://github.com/fnick6800/WIPoker/releases/latest)

## English

- Improved endgame cooperation when a partner plays a full house and has only one or two cards left.
- Reduced unnecessary full-house takeovers that leave the computer with a weak single and interrupt its partner's finish.
- Retained exceptions for blocking an opponent's immediate finish and safe finishing routes. Hints and auto-play use the same correction.

Android 7.0+. Build 100. Install over your existing app to keep your local account and progress; do not uninstall first.
