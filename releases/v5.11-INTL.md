# WIPoker · In-game voice selection

The card-call voice list opens directly under the current voice in your profile and the first-time Guandan guide. Scroll through nine voices, tap one to select it, and use the speaker button to hear it again. Confirm your first choice to enter the Guandan lobby.

Android 7.0+. Build 103. Install over the existing version to keep your local progress.

## 游戏内音色选择

个人资料和首次进入掼蛋时，点当前音色即可在下方展开列表。每种性别有九种声音，选中后自动收起，扬声器按钮可重听。首次选声后点击确认即可进入掼蛋大厅。

Android 7.0 及以上，安装序号 103。请直接覆盖安装以保留存档。
