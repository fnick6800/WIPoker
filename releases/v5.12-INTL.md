# Guandan partner play · 掼蛋队友配合

When your partner leads a straight, computer players now recognize their teammate correctly from all four seats. They can save a bomb while the team holds the trick and use it if an opponent takes over.

掼蛋中，电脑在四个座位都能正确识别打出顺子的队友。队友领牌时可以留住炸弹，对手用更大顺子接走后仍可争回牌权。

Android 7.0+ · Build 104. Install over your existing version to keep local progress.
支持 Android 7.0 及以上。直接覆盖安装即可保留本地进度。
