# WIPoker · Guandan motion and audio update

[English](https://github.com/fnick6800/WIPoker/blob/main/README.md) · [简体中文](https://github.com/fnick6800/WIPoker/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md) · [Download APK](https://github.com/fnick6800/WIPoker/releases/latest)

## English

- Reduced rendering work during Guandan card plays and trick collection, and unified movement and scaling for multi-card plays.
- Added card-gathering sounds, with pause/resume and sound settings respected.
- Shortened pauses between “pair” and the rank in selected Keke voice announcements, preserving the original voice.
- Fixed a specific Guandan hint/AI case that spent wild cards on a rank bomb while overlooking a stronger straight flush with a much simpler remaining hand.

Android 7.0+. Install the APK over your existing app to keep local accounts and progress; do not uninstall first. Build 99. Device performance can vary.
