# WIPoker · Guandan endgame teamwork

When you have one or two cards left, your partner gives you a chance to keep the lead. A low single or pair can still be followed naturally. If an opponent takes over, your partner can use a saved bomb to win the lead back. Computer players, hints and auto-play share this change.

Android 7.0+. Build 101. Install over the existing app to keep your local progress.

## 掼蛋残局配合

你只剩一两张牌时，电脑队友优先让你保住牌权。明显的小单张、小对子仍可顺手跟出。对手接过牌权后，队友可用保留的炸弹争回。电脑出牌、提示和代打同步更新。

Android 7.0 及以上，安装序号 101。直接覆盖安装可保留本地进度。
