# WIPoker · Guandan voices and auto-play

Choose a card-call voice by character and sound in the new dropdown. Selecting a voice previews it, and the speaker button replays the sample. Pair calls now have shorter pauses between the call and the rank.

Guandan auto-play stays on between rounds. With automatic next round enabled, the scoreboard stays up for about three seconds before the next deal. Offline play pauses in the background and resumes when you return.

Android 7.0+. Build 102. Install over your existing version to keep your account and progress.

## 掼蛋报牌与代打

从单行下拉菜单选择牌友报牌音色，选择时可试听，点击扬声器可重听。缩短部分对子播报中“对”和牌点之间的停顿。

代打可连续进行多局。开启自动下一局后，结算展示约三秒后发牌。单机牌局退到后台暂停，回到前台继续。

Android 7.0 及以上，安装序号102。请直接覆盖安装以保留存档。
